feat(nav): unify English navigation into SiteHeader component

All 24 English pages previously carried their own inline copy of the
navbar, spread across 7 mutually inconsistent wording variants
(In-Depth / Deep Analysis, About & Contact / About & Collaboration, …).
They now render from the single SiteHeader component, alongside the 55
Chinese pages, so a navbar edit is once again a one-file change.

Nav wording is aligned one-to-one with the Chinese nav:
  Home / Deep Analysis / Case Studies / About Me & Business
with the fourth item pointing at ../about-en.html.

Component changes:
  - add hrefPrefix      so /en/ pages can emit ../ relative links
  - add aboutHref       so the English About item targets the EN page
  - keep an explicit __frozen escape hatch for
    en/article-manufacturing-ai-ontology, whose header layout is
    structurally unlike every other page and cannot round-trip through
    the general template. Its nav wording is unified; only the layout
    stays verbatim.

extract-header.mjs now scans src/sources recursively and gained a
nav-scope gate: nav rows, logo href and logo img src are normalized and
compared byte-for-byte, and any page that would change outside that
scope is rejected rather than rewritten.

verify.mjs gained a whitelist-by-subtraction check for the English
navigation: nav rows, the lang button and logo hrefs are discounted and
the remainder must still match the archive exactly, which keeps the
whitelist itself under test.

The 24 stale pre-migration public/en/*.html snapshots are deleted; the
built site serves the English pages from dist/en/ via Astro.

Verification: verify.mjs reports 90/90 pages matching the archive.
Changing a single prop (brand) in the component and rebuilding altered
79 pages (55 zh + 24 en), where previously the English pages would not
have reacted at all. That probe was reverted and the diff returns to 0.
