05 / FAQ

How is an AI Agent different from ChatGPT?+

A general AI mainly answers questions. An enterprise AI Agent can connect to internal data, understand business rules, call system tools to execute tasks, generate reports, trigger approval flows, and improve through feedback. The goal is a system that does work, not a chatbot.

Our data is scattered — can AI still be connected?+

Yes. Most enterprises run ERP, CRM, spreadsheets, Feishu, and databases side by side. The work starts with data ingestion → cleansing → business modeling → AI applications. Perfect data is not a prerequisite.

Will our business data leak?+

Private deployment, on-premise servers, local models, and access controls are all supported. Core business data does not have to pass through third-party public platforms.

Why not just buy SaaS software?+

SaaS solves generic needs, but real enterprise processes are full of variation — different ERPs, approval flows, and business rules. Enterprise AI needs to work with existing systems rather than add another isolated tool.
